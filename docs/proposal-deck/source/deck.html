Swiss Native·Product proposal·Guest17

Book a tour, hold seats for 15 minutes

The guest confirms contact details and pays the amount due today, and the seats stay held for 15 minutes while the payment is completed.

15-minute seat hold

The clock starts when the guest continues to payment, keeps running after a reload and is not restarted by a failed attempt.

Payment rule per departure

Each date is set to full payment, a 30% deposit with the balance later, or booking now and paying later.

Booked is not paid

Retry in the same window

Expiry releases the seats

When the hold ends the booking becomes Expired, its seats are released once, and the payment can no longer be made.

Rebook from the same page

Check availability again reopens the departure sheet with the group kept, and seats and prices are re-checked.

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Shop31

Change the date or place, or decline

When the requested pickup does not work, the shop can agree a new date or place with the guest, or decline with a reason the guest receives.

Only valid dates offered

The calendar greys out past days and Sundays, when both pickup places are closed. A note to the guest is required.

Still a request

A changed order stays unconfirmed until the shop accepts it, so the guest never pays for a date nobody confirmed.

Decline with a reason

Pick a reason and add an optional message. The guest gets both by email, and other orders are unaffected.

No overpayment

Money to collect

Result per booking

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Shop37

One page holds everything about a tour booking: what the guest agreed to, money received and owed, and the next step for the team.

Terms as booked

Price, payment rule and cancellation policy are saved at booking, so later tour changes never alter this booking.

Overdue balances

Each departure's saved rule decides: cancel and release seats after the deadline, allow a grace period, or leave it to you.

Strict completion

External payments only

The sheet records money already received outside the platform, up to the amount still owed, by cash, bank transfer or other.

Complete asks to confirm

Full history kept

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Shop39

What happens when a balance is late

Each departure saves its own rule for unpaid balances at booking time. The shop sees the result without chasing each case.

Cancel and release seats

Under this rule the booking is cancelled at the next check after the deadline, and the seats are free again.

Grace period

Another rule waits a set time, such as 48 hours, and shows the date until which the guest may still pay.

Paid money is kept

Cancellation keeps what was already paid on record. Refunds are not part of this version of the product.

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Shop40

Partners are hotels, guides or others who send you guests; each gets a personal link and QR code so you can see what their guests buy.

Search and filter partners

Find a partner by name, code or email, or show only those you still owe commission.

Link opens, never 'scans'

Adding a partner takes four fields; the shop then gets a QR code and link to hand out, and every guest who opens it is credited.

Four fields

Name, type (hotel, guide or other), the email that receives report codes, and a short referral code.

QR code to download

Save the QR code as a PNG for flyers or desks, or copy the link to share online.

Credit for 30 days

A guest who opens the link sees the normal home page; their orders in the next 30 days count for the partner.

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Partners42

Commissions and payouts, fully tracked

The shop records each payment to a partner, and the partner sees the same figures in a private report without any guest contact details.

Pay what is owed

The amount to pay starts at what is still owed after offsets; the Owner or Manager picks method, date and a reference.

Offsets are not payments

If a refund lowers commission already paid, the next commissions are offset first and never shown as money paid.

Partner's own report

Day, week or month totals, commission paid and owed, with guest names only and no email, phone or notes.

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Partners43

Refund offsets and report link control

When a refund cuts commission you already paid, the shop owes nothing until later commissions cover it, and the Owner can cut off a partner's report.

Never a negative payout

Hotel am See shows CHF 0.00 to pay and CHF 11.10 to offset; payments restart once later commissions cover it.

Ledger shows each step

Every sale, offset and payout appears in a dated list, newest first.

Revoke needs confirmation

Only the Owner can revoke a report link; it stops working at once on every device, and a new one can follow.

Swiss Native·Product proposal·Partners44

How a partner opens their report

Partners need no account: a private link plus a six-digit code sent to their email opens a report that only they can see.

Link plus emailed code

The page shows only a masked email; Send code mails a six-digit code that works for 10 minutes.

Wrong codes are limited

A wrong code shows an error and how many tries are left; too many asks for a new code.

Kept for 30 days

After the code, the same browser reopens the report directly for 30 days; a forwarded link still asks for a code.

Paid, refunded, offset

By product group

Orders without contact data

Best sellers

Products, variants and tours ranked, with stock and seat figures beside them.

Operations

Delivered, no-show and cancelled counts, plus how stories and the advice chat perform.

Margin is only shown for sales with every cost entered, so the shop fills in the gaps, and the report updates at once.

Five cost types

Cost of goods, packaging, payment fee, delivery and other variable costs, per completed order or booking.

Empty is missing, not zero

An empty field stays flagged; the shop enters an amount or ticks that it doesn't apply (0).
